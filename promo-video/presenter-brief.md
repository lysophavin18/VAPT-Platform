## Spoken script

Every digital service creates opportunity. It also creates something your organization needs to protect. Websites, applications, and connected assets can develop security weaknesses that are difficult to track across separate tools.

Meet NoovaStack VAPT: a centralized workspace designed to help security teams organize assessments, investigate findings, and make better-informed decisions.

Start with visibility. Bring projects and assets together, define the scope of an engagement, and keep authorized testing organized. Your team gains a shared view of what needs attention and where assessments stand.

Next, manage your assessment workflow. Review scans, follow progress, and explore technical findings from one interface. Evidence and vulnerability details help analysts investigate potential issues and decide what requires further verification.

Vulnerability information changes every day. NoovaStack connects its assessment workflow with CVE intelligence and Nuclei-based detection. Scheduled update jobs are designed to refresh NVD information, CISA exploitation context, and Nuclei templates, helping teams keep emerging risks in view.

When a finding needs explanation, AI assistance can help analysts understand the issue and explore remediation options. Human review remains central to interpreting evidence and deciding the next step.

Security testing also needs accountability. Scope controls, approval workflows, and audit trails support authorized assessments. Teams can coordinate their work while keeping oversight visible.

From the dashboard to individual findings, NoovaStack brings the security assessment process into a clearer, more connected workspace.

For organizations building their security capabilities, that means a practical place to start, investigate, and improve.

NoovaStack VAPT. Clearer visibility. Better-informed security decisions.
